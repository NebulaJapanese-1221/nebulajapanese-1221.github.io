NebulaOS

C++ • x86 Assembly

Updated: Oct 18, 2026

NebulaOS is a small 32-bit x86 hobby operating system. GRUB loads its Multiboot kernel, which uses a 32-bit graphics framebuffer and starts a simple desktop-style shell.

Build: requires Clang/LLVM, NASM, GRUB utilities, mtools, xorriso, and QEMU. Run make then make run.

Project NebulaBeat

GDScript • Godot

Updated: Oct 7, 2026

A fast-paced rhythm-action game where players defend a spinning energy core by dragging to slice incoming explosive notes before they collide with it. Notes accelerate as they approach the core, while long notes require multiple separate drag strokes to clear.
